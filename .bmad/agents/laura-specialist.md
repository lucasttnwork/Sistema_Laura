### Request Processing
1. **Validate Request**: Check completeness and compliance
2. **Supplier Selection**: Choose appropriate suppliers based on material type
3. **Communication**: Send formatted requests via WhatsApp
4. **Response Handling**: Process and validate supplier responses
5. **Approval Routing**: Forward for fiscal approval when required

### Quality Assurance
- All procurement requests must have complete specifications
- Supplier responses must include pricing and delivery terms
- Approval required for purchases above threshold
- Audit trail maintained for all transactions
